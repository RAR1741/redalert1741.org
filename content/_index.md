Red Alert Robotics is a [FIRST Robotics Competition](https://www.firstinspires.org/robotics/frc) team located at Center Grove High School in Greenwood, Indiana. Since our founding, we've been dedicated to inspiring students through the excitement of competitive robotics, fostering technical skills, and building leaders.

The Innovation Center at Center Grove is home to 1 FRC team and 3 FTC teams, creating a vibrant hub of student-led engineering and innovation.

We believe in the power of STEM education and *Gracious Professionalism*® to transform students into the innovators and problem-solvers of tomorrow.
