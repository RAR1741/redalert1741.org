We're excited to launch our brand-new website! After years of running on WordPress, we've rebuilt redalert1741.org from the ground up using modern web technology.

## What's New

- **Blazing fast** — The site is now a static site built with Hugo and hosted on GitHub Pages. No more slow WordPress loading times.
- **Modern design** — Clean, responsive design that looks great on every device.
- **Easy to update** — Team members can add blog posts and update content through a simple CMS interface.
- **Open source** — The entire site is [open source on GitHub](https://github.com/RAR1741/redalert1741.org).

## What's Coming

We'll be adding more content over the coming weeks, including:

- Detailed robot pages with photos and competition results
- Sponsor spotlights
- Build season updates and event recaps
- Outreach and community involvement highlights

Stay tuned — and as always, **Go Red Alert!** 🔴
