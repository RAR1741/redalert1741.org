A.N.D.Y. was Red Alert's robot for the 2015 FRC season, *Recycle Rush*. This unique game challenged robots to stack totes on scoring platforms, cap the stacks with recycling containers, and properly dispose of pool noodles ("litter").

Recycle Rush was distinctive as a co-operative game — there were no direct interactions between opposing alliances, with a step dividing the field. This allowed teams to focus purely on their stacking and scoring strategies.
