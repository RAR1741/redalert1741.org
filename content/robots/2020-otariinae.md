## The Game

*FIRST Infinite Recharge* challenged robots to collect and launch Power Cells (yellow foam balls) into goals while navigating a color-spinning control panel and climbing at the end of the match.

## The Robot

Otariinae returned for the 2020 season, sharing its name with the 2018 version and reflecting the team's design lineage and continuous improvement philosophy.

The 2020 season was unfortunately cut short due to the COVID-19 pandemic, but the team was able to compete at early events before the worldwide shutdown.

The team won the **District Chairman's Award** at the Bloomington District and the **Chairman's Award** at the Indiana State Championship before the rest of the season was suspended.
