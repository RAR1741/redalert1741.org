## The Game

*Overdrive* was the game for 2008. The field was divided lengthwise by a fence median to create a track with red and blue zones on either side. The fence was crossed by an overpass marking the red and blue finish lines. The overpass also held the game pieces called "Trackballs." Robots raced around the track in a counter-clockwise direction while manipulating the trackballs to score points in various ways.

## The Robot

Thaddeus was designed to pick up a 4ft ball with a vacuum. We won the **Motorola Creativity Award** at the Boilermaker Regional because our vacuum was actually a cleverly used cake cover. The team also received the **Xerox Creativity Award** at the St. Louis Regional.
