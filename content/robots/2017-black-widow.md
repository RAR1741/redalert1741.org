Black Widow was Red Alert's entry for the 2017 FRC season, *FIRST Steamworks*. Set in a steampunk universe, robots worked together to generate steam pressure by delivering fuel (wiffle balls) and gears, then climbing a rope to board an airship at the end of the match.

Black Widow was designed with a focus on reliable gear delivery and consistent climbing, key strategies for success in the 2017 game.
