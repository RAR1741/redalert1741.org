Knight Fury was Red Alert's robot for the 2016 FRC season, *FIRST Stronghold*. In this medieval-themed game, alliances worked to breach their opponents' outer defenses, score boulders in the opposing tower, and eventually capture the tower.

The game featured an array of unique defenses that robots needed to cross, requiring versatile drivetrains and creative mechanisms. Knight Fury was built to tackle these challenges head-on.
