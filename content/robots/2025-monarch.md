## The Game

*REEFSCAPE℠ presented by Haas* challenges alliances of three robots to score coral on a multi-level reef structure, process algae, and navigate the ocean floor. In the endgame, robots attempt to ascend to different levels of a cage structure for bonus points.

## The Robot

Monarch is Red Alert's entry for the 2025 FRC season. More details and competition results will be added as the season progresses.
