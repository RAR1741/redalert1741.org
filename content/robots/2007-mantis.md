## The Game

The goal of *Rack 'n Roll* was to take inner-tube shaped game pieces and place them onto an octagonal centerpiece in the middle of the field. The centerpiece was placed in a random position to increase the difficulty of the autonomous period. In the end game, all of the rules remained the same, except that robots could not enter their opponent's zone.

## The Robot

Mantis could lift and place inner tubes on racks with a claw used to grip the inner tubes. The team attended the Wisconsin Regional and the Boilermaker Regional during the 2007 season.
