## Support Red Alert Robotics!

Our team relies on the generous support of sponsors to build robots, compete, and inspire the next generation of STEM leaders. Your partnership makes a real difference in the lives of our students.

### Your generous gift allows our program to:

- **Inspire** young people to be science and technology leaders.
- **Engage** them in exciting mentor-based programs.
- **Build** science, engineering, and technology skills in students.
- **Foster** well-rounded life skills including self-confidence, communication, and leadership.

Together, as a Robotics sponsor, we can continue to transform our culture by creating a world where science and technology are celebrated and where young people dream of becoming science and technology leaders.

---

### Tax-Deductible Donations

1741 Red Alert Robotics is a non-profit organization, so **any donations that you make are tax-deductible!**

[Contact us](mailto:president@redalert1741.org) to learn how you can become a sponsor and support our team.
