## Meet Our Team

Red Alert Robotics is driven by a passionate group of high school students from Center Grove High School who come together to design, build, program, and compete with robots. Our students gain real-world experience in engineering, programming, business, marketing, and community outreach — skills that prepare them for college and careers in STEM and beyond.

![2024 Red Alert Team Photo](/images/team-photo-2024.jpg)

---

## Team Leadership Structure

Our team is organized into specialized sub-teams, each led by student captains who work together to ensure every aspect of the robot and team operations runs smoothly. This structure gives students the opportunity to develop leadership skills, take ownership of their work, and collaborate across disciplines.
