## Welcome to RARPO!

<div class="bg-rally-50 border border-rally-200 rounded-xl p-6 md:p-8 not-prose mb-8">
  <p class="text-lg text-gray-700 leading-relaxed">
    As with all things robotics, we have our own acronym — <strong class="text-rally-600">RARPO</strong> (Red Alert Robotics Parent Organization). We are the community of parental units that help support the awesome work done by our kids.
  </p>
</div>

**RARPO** is a **501(c)(3) organization** made up of parents of the students on FIRST Team 1741 Red Alert Robotics and associated FTC teams. Our mission is to **support the team with fundraising and all other forms of support** necessary to keep the program thriving.

Whether you're a seasoned robotics parent or brand new to FIRST, there's a place for you in RARPO. We're all in this together — supporting our students as they learn, build, and compete.
